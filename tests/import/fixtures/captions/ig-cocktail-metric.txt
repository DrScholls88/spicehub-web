AVIATION 💜 the prettiest gin cocktail nobody orders

4.5 cl gin
1.5 cl fresh lemon juice
1.5 cl maraschino liqueur
1 cl creme de violette (optional, for the color)

Shake everything hard with ice for a full 15 seconds. Double strain into a chilled coupe glass. Garnish with a brandied cherry.

Save this for your next dinner party 🍸 #aviation #ginlover #cocktailhour #mixology
