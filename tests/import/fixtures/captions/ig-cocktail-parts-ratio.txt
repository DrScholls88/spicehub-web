NEGRONI SEASON IS HERE 🍊🥃 equal parts, zero excuses

1 part gin
1 part Campari
1 part sweet vermouth

Stir all three with plenty of ice in a mixing glass until well chilled, about 30 seconds. Strain into a rocks glass over one big cube. Express an orange peel over the top and drop it in.

Cheers 🍹 #negroni #classiccocktails #homebartender #apertivo
