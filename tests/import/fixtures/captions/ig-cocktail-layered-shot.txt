B-52 layered shot 🔥🔥🔥 who remembers these from the 90s

1/3 Kahlua
1/3 Baileys Irish Cream
1/3 Grand Marnier

Pour the Kahlua in first. Layer the Baileys over the back of a bar spoon so it floats. Layer the Grand Marnier on top the same way. Serve immediately with a straw or shoot it.
