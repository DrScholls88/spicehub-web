Smoked Maple Sour

2 oz Bourbon (or rye)
3/4 oz Lemon juice, fresh
1⁄2 oz Maple syrup (grade A dark)
1/4 oz Smoked simple syrup
2 ds Angostura bitters
1 ea Egg white (optional)
1 twst Orange peel (as garnish)
Instructions

Combine bourbon, lemon juice, both syrups, bitters and egg white in a shaker and dry-shake hard for 10 seconds.

Add ice, shake until well chilled, and double-strain into a chilled coupe. Express the orange peel over the top and drop it in.
