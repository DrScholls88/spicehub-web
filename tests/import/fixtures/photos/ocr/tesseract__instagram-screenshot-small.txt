simplecookingwithpep This Easy Lasagna Soup is just
that...easy! Let's face it, making lasagna can be time
consuming, but it's always worth the effort, because
it's so good. Enter this Easy Lasagna Soup and you can
have all of the delicious cozy flavors of lasagna, but
with minimal effort. This soup is made with a mix of
vegetables and beef simmered in an herby tomato
sauce and tender lasagna noodles. It is topped with a
dollop of creamy ricotta blended with parmesan and
mozzarella cheese and fresh parsley. Enjoy with your
favorite crusty bread for an easy, cozy weeknight meal!
Easy Lasagna Soup
1T olive oil
11b lean ground beef
1 medium yellow onion, chopped
1 red bell pepper
Salt and pepper
2 tsp garlic, minced
1(28 oz) can crushed tomatoes
